ArcGis¶

  • ArcGis

  • ArcGIS Earth / Online:

    • A downloadable application similar to Google Earth that integrates with other Esri tools. It supports 3D city views, imports various data formats, and includes professional-grade tools for analysis.

  • Esri

    • Esri ArcGIS / ArcGIS Earth: A powerful suite of GIS tools, including ArcGIS Earth, a downloadable program offering 3D city views and access to a wide range of data layers. Professional GIS & Data Analysis

Nasa¶

  • World View About WorldView Legion

    • Excellent for exploring global, full-resolution satellite imagery and downloading data, ideal for monitoring environmental changes.

    • This app from NASA’s Earth Observing System Data and Information System (EOSDIS) allows for interactive browsing of full-resolution imagery in near real-time, often within three hours of retrieval. It is especially useful for tracking environmental events.

  • Earth Data

    • The Earth Science Data Systems (ESDS) Program provides open access to NASA’s archive of Earth science data, empowering researchers and decision makers to better understand and protect our home planet.

  • Esto

  • Esto Quantum

ESA¶

  • Copernicus (Data Space Ecosystem):

    • This platform provides free access to all Sentinel satellite data from the European Space Agency (ESA). It includes optical, radar, and other data for environmental monitoring and offers powerful visualization and processing tools.

  • Zoom Earth:

    • Features the most recent satellite images, aerial views, and real-time weather tracking for storms and other phenomena.

    • Offers near real-time satellite views that are updated every 10–15 minutes. It pulls imagery from weather satellites operated by organizations like NOAA and provides instant updates on weather, storms, and wildfires.

  • Mapillary

    • A platform for accessing and contributing street-level imagery, helping to keep maps up-to-date with details not visible from aerial views.

    • This platform makes street-level images and map data available by using crowdsourced imagery from users. The images are licensed under a Creative Commons license, providing a community-driven alternative to Google Street View.

  • QGis

    • A free and open-source Geographic Information System (GIS) that serves as a robust alternative for geospatial analysis.

    • A free and open-source desktop Geographic Information System (GIS) application that supports viewing, editing, and analyzing geospatial data.

  • SkyFi:

    • Focuses on providing daily, high-resolution satellite images, offering a much more current view of Earth than Google Earth’s typically older images. Specialized Imagery Provider

    • A platform that offers on-demand satellite images. Unlike Google Earth’s archival imagery, SkyFi provides more recent images from a variety of satellites. Users can request and purchase high-resolution images of a specific location and date.

  • Nearmap / TerraServer / Vantor DigitalGlobe:

    • Historical satellite imagery providers that can be useful for specific timeframes and high-resolution needs.

  • USGS EarthExplorer:

    • The U.S. Geological Survey offers a vast catalog of free satellite and aerial imagery, some dating back over 40 years. While not as user-friendly for amateurs, it’s an excellent resource for researchers needing a variety of space-derived data.

  • EOSDA LandViewer:

    • A GIS database that provides free access to current and historical satellite imagery, along with analytical tools. It is popular among GIS experts and beginners in fields like agriculture and environmental protection.

  • OpenStreetMap.org (OSM):

    • A collaborative, open-source project that creates a free, editable world map. It relies on a global community of mappers and is an excellent alternative to proprietary map data.

  • HERE WeGo:

    • Formerly known as HERE Maps, this app is a solid alternative to Google Maps for general navigation. It provides features like offline maps, route planning, and public transit information.

  • Mapbox:

    • A popular mapping platform for developers that offers customizable maps and navigation. Its satellite imagery is often sourced from the same providers as other mapping services.

  • Global Mapper / Maptitude / MapInfo Pro:

    • Professional desktop software for advanced geospatial analysis and mapping.
